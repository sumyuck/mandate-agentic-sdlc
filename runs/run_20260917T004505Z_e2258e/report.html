Native SQLite serialization + unique constraint + bounded retry — It gives the exact correctness AC19 demands (a DB-level constraint that cannot be raced) with the least code, matches the single-instance scope stated in the NFRs precisely, and needs no additional locking primitives — at the cost of write throughput being bounded by SQLite's single-writer model and a small, well-understood retry surface for the alias/generated-code collision case.
